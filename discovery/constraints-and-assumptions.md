# Constraints and Assumptions

## Constraints
- Prototype timeline: 2 weeks
- Historical data is limited
- Hosted AI is not required for the lab
- Customer requires explainable routing
- Integration should be API-first

## Assumptions
- Ticket text contains enough information for initial routing.
- Customer can provide team definitions.
- Humans remain responsible during pilot.
- Prototype may begin with deterministic and simulated-AI behavior.
