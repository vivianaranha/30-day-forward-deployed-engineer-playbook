# Success Metrics

## Business
- Reduce median triage time by at least 40%
- Reduce manual routing actions by at least 50%
- Maintain or improve routing accuracy
- Reduce avoidable ticket reassignments

## Technical
- Pilot availability >= 99%
- p95 response time < 3 seconds
- Invalid-request handling 100%
- Critical-ticket recall target >= 95%

## Adoption
- Pilot users actively use the tool
- At least 70% of recommendations accepted or judged useful
