# Discovery Question Bank

## Business
- What problem are you trying to solve?
- Why does this matter now?
- How is it handled today?
- What is the business cost?
- What would success look like in 30, 60, and 90 days?

## Users
- Who performs the workflow today?
- Who consumes the output?
- Who approves decisions?
- What frustrates users most?

## Process
- Walk me through one real example.
- Where are manual handoffs?
- Where are delays introduced?
- What happens when something goes wrong?

## Data
- What sources are available?
- Which fields are trustworthy?
- Is historical data available?
- What is sensitive?

## Technology
- What systems are involved?
- Are APIs available?
- What authentication is required?
- What environments exist?

## Constraints
- Security?
- Data residency?
- Time-to-value?
- Performance?
- Budget?

## Success
- What metric should improve?
- What baseline exists?
- How will value be measured?
