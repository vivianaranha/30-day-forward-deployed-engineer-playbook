# Customer Scenario

## Customer
Northstar Support Services

## Stakeholder
VP of Customer Support

## Initial request
> “Can AI help us automate support ticket routing?”

## What an FDE should hear
This is not yet a requirement. It is a hypothesis. Discovery must determine what is slow today, who does the work, how much it costs, what systems are involved, which data is trustworthy, and what level of automation is acceptable.
