# FDE Engagement Brief

## Customer
Northstar Support Services

## Problem
Manual support-ticket triage consumes too much time and creates inconsistent routing.

## Users
Support agents and support managers.

## Pilot scope
Category, priority, summary, team recommendation, user feedback.

## Success
Faster triage, fewer manual routing touches, lower reassignments, strong user acceptance.

## Out of scope
Autonomous ticket closure, automated customer communication, production SLA commitments.
